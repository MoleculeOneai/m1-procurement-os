# Gotchas — Supplier Management

Add a line each time Claude trips on something new.

---

## Due Diligence Gotchas

- **Do not claim to have run checks.** Sanctions screening, credit checks and adverse-media searches need real data sources. Record only results the user supplies, with the date and source. Otherwise list the check as outstanding.
- **"Not evidenced" is not "pass".** A supplier who did not return the questionnaire has not passed security review.
- **Proportionality.** Enhanced due diligence on a low-value commodity supplier slows the business and gets bypassed. Match depth to value, data access and criticality.
- **Beware stale evidence.** Certificates and financial statements expire or age. Record the dates and flag anything older than 12 months.
- **Check the legal entity.** The entity that signs and invoices may differ from the brand the user knows. Confirm the contracting entity.
- **Fourth parties.** The supplier's subcontractors and hosting providers carry risk too. Ask who they rely on for the critical service.

## Risk Gotchas

- **Do not rate a named company from memory.** Financial strength, litigation and ownership change. Use supplied data and recommend the right source.
- **Scores need reasons.** A bare 4 × 3 means nothing. Give a one-line basis for severity and likelihood.
- **Concentration is not always bad.** Consolidation can be deliberate. Flag it as a risk only when there is no tested alternative or switch time exceeds the tolerable outage.
- **Do not mix controls with risks.** "No SOC 2 report" is a finding. The risk is what could happen because of it.
- **Accepted risk needs an owner.** Accepting without a name and review date means ignoring.

## Relationship Gotchas

- **Do not tier on spend alone.** A low-spend supplier can be critical to operations. Include criticality and replaceability.
- **Keep internal notes internal.** Review packs shared with the supplier must not contain targets, fallback positions or leverage assessments.
- **Remediation needs a decision point.** An improvement plan without a date to decide continue or exit drifts indefinitely.
- **An exit with no alternative is a bluff.** Confirm that a ready replacement exists before planning an exit.
