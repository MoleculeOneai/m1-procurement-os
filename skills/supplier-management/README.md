# Supplier Management
**Molecule One Procurement OS — Supplier Lifecycle**

Onboard suppliers with proportionate due diligence, keep a scored risk register, and manage relationships by tier.

## What it does
- Due-diligence checklists at Light, Standard and Enhanced levels, with an approve / conditions / reject decision
- Supplier risk register using severity-by-likelihood scoring and mitigation owners
- Concentration and single-source analysis from a spend list
- Supplier tiering, business-review packs, development plans and exit plans

## Usage examples
```
"Run due diligence on a new payroll provider — they will hold employee data"
"Build a risk register for our top 20 suppliers — spend list attached"
"Which of our suppliers are single-source and what is the exposure?"
"Prepare the quarterly business review for our logistics partner"
```

## Works with
`supplier-scorecard-engine`, `contract-lifecycle-manager`, `esg-sustainability-assessor`, `category-strategy-builder`, `spend-analyzer`.

## Notes
Does not run credit, sanctions or adverse-media checks itself. It tells you which to run and records the results you provide. Adapted in part from Anthropic's `vendor-management` and `risk-assessment` skills (Apache License 2.0).
