# Molecule One — Branding Reference for Document Generation

## Document Headers
- Organisation: use `org_name` from config.json
- All documents include: "Part of the Molecule One Claude Plugin Suite"
- Footer: "Confidential — [ORG_NAME] Procurement | moleculeone.ai"

## Tone of Voice
- Direct and professional — no filler phrases ("please note that...", "it should be mentioned...")
- Active voice — "The strategy targets" not "Savings are expected to be achieved by"
- Plain language — no jargon without explanation
- Precise — use specific amounts, dates, and percentages; avoid vague language like "significant savings"
- Assertive without being overconfident — use "central estimate" not "guaranteed savings"

## Colour Palette (for Word document generation)
- Primary navy: #1B3A6B
- Accent blue: #2E75B6
- Light blue (table headers): #D5E8F0
- Body text: #1A1A1A
- Warning / flag highlight: #FFF3CD (amber background for [REVIEW NEEDED] items)

## Terminology Standards
- "Supplier" not "vendor" (unless the client uses vendor)
- "Proposal" not "bid" or "tender response"
- "Evaluation" not "assessment" or "scoring exercise"
- "Award" not "win" — procurement awards contracts, suppliers don't win them
- "Shortlist" not "short-list" or "final list"
- "Total Cost of Ownership" or "TCO" — always spell out on first use
- "Savings opportunity" not "savings target" — until formally committed
- "Spend" not "expenditure" or "purchasing spend" (redundant)
- "Category" not "commodity" (unless it is literally a commodity market)
- "Incumbent" not "existing supplier" — cleaner and standard in procurement
- "Tender" only in jurisdictions where this is standard legal terminology; otherwise "RFP" or "competitive process"
- "Kraljic" (capital K) — it is a proper noun (after Peter Kraljic)

## Document Naming Convention
- Category Strategy: [ORG_NAME]-Category-Strategy-[CATEGORY]-[YYYY].docx
- Kraljic Matrix: [ORG_NAME]-Kraljic-Matrix-[YYYY].docx
- Market Brief: [ORG_NAME]-Market-Brief-[CATEGORY]-[YYYY].docx
- Opportunity Register: [ORG_NAME]-Opportunity-Register-[CATEGORY]-[YYYY].docx

## Numbers & Formatting
- Currency amounts: use symbols ($, £, €) not words; use commas for thousands ($1,250,000)
- Percentages: always include % symbol; no decimal for round numbers (15%, not 15.0%)
- Dates: use ISO format in data tables (2025-03-15); use DD MMM YYYY in documents (15 Mar 2025)
- Savings ranges: always present as Conservative / Central / Optimistic — never a single point estimate
- Confidence levels: High / Medium / Low (capitalised, not high/medium/low)
- Effort levels: H / M / L in tables; "High", "Medium", "Low" in narrative
