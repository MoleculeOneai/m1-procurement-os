## Scoring Guidance

**Scale:** 1 (Unacceptable) → 2 → 3 (Acceptable) → 4 → 5 (Excellent)
**Even scores (2 and 4):** Use these to position between anchor points.
**N/A:** Use only when data is genuinely unavailable for the period. N/A dimensions are excluded from the weighted total — document why the data was unavailable.
**Do not guess:** If you don't have supporting data, mark N/A. A guessed score is worse than no score.
