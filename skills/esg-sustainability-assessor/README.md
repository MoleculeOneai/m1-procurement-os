# ESG and Sustainability Assessor
**Molecule One Procurement OS — Sustainable Procurement**

Assess supplier ESG, screen supply-chain emissions from spend, and turn regulatory duties into procurement actions.

## What it does
- Risk-based supplier ESG screening and evidence-based scoring with red-flag escalation
- Tailored supplier questionnaires and improvement plans
- Spend-based Scope 3 screening using your emission factors, with hotspot ranking
- Mapping of the rules you say apply to procurement tasks, tender criteria and contract clauses

## Usage examples
```
"Assess our top 15 suppliers on ESG — here is the spend list"
"Estimate the emissions hotspots in our purchased goods — spend file attached"
"What do we need to do in procurement for our supply chain due diligence duties?"
"Write ESG criteria and weights for our packaging RFP"
```

## Works with
`spend-analyzer`, `supplier-management`, `rfp-generator`, `rfp-response-evaluator`, `contract-lifecycle-manager`, `category-strategy-builder`.

## Notes
Does not supply emission factors or state what law applies to you. You provide the factors and Legal confirms applicability. Spend-based results are screening estimates, not a reportable inventory.
