# Gotchas — ESG and Sustainability Assessor

Add a line each time Claude trips on something new.

---

## Evidence Gotchas

- **Self-declared is not verified.** Mark every answer as self-declared, certificate-backed or third-party-audited. Score unevidenced answers 0, not average.
- **Certificates have scope and expiry.** A site certificate does not cover other sites or the whole company. Record scope and expiry date.
- **Do not rate named companies from memory.** ESG ratings, emissions and controversies change. Use documents the user supplies and name the source and date.
- **A red-flag finding overrides the average.** Evidence of forced labour or serious breach must not be diluted by good scores elsewhere.
- **Do not send the same questionnaire to everyone.** Screen by inherent risk and tailor questions. Long generic questionnaires produce low response and low-quality answers.

## Emissions Gotchas

- **Never invent emission factors.** Use only factors the user provides, and record source, year and currency basis. With no factors, produce a hotspot ranking and say what is needed.
- **Spend-based is a screen, not an inventory.** Label every spend-based figure "indicative". Price inflation and currency moves distort it.
- **Convert currency and year before applying factors.** Factors are tied to a currency and base year.
- **Exclude non-emitting spend and avoid double counting.** Taxes, interest and intercompany do not belong. Capital goods are reported separately from purchased goods and services.
- **Do not promise reductions.** State reduction ranges only if the user supplies the basis.
- **External reporting needs governance.** Anything used for a public disclosure goes through Sustainability and Finance under the reporting method and assurance process.

## Regulatory Gotchas

- **Do not state thresholds, dates or penalties from memory.** These rules have been revised and rescoped repeatedly. Tell the user to confirm current status with Legal or an official source.
- **Applicability is a legal question.** Record who confirmed it. Mark unconfirmed items as such.
- **Separate law from customer demands and voluntary commitments.** They carry different consequences and negotiating room.
- **Clause drafts are starting points.** Mark them for Legal review. Never describe them as compliant.
- **Cascade to subcontractors.** A duty on the buyer often needs flow-down terms and data rights from suppliers. A policy alone does not deliver it.
