# Mode: Draft a New RFI

## Goal
Generate a Request for Information to gather supplier capability data before committing to a full RFP. RFIs are exploratory — they do not commit the buyer to any procurement action.

## Key Differences vs RFP
- No pricing schedule (RFIs do not solicit pricing)
- No formal evaluation scoring — outputs are a capability matrix, not a ranked shortlist
- Shorter document (target: 4–8 pages max)
- Broader supplier distribution — RFIs are often sent to 8–15 suppliers, not just a shortlist
- Explicitly state: "This RFI does not constitute a commitment to purchase"

## Step-by-Step

**Step 1 — Clarify purpose.**
Ask: "What decision will the RFI inform?" Common answers: build a supplier longlist, assess market maturity, understand pricing benchmarks (ballpark only), identify capability gaps.

**Step 2 — Draft RFI with these sections:**
1. Background & Context (what you're trying to solve)
2. Company overview questions (size, locations, certifications)
3. Capability questions (specific to category — ask user to confirm)
4. Case study / reference request (max 2)
5. Indicative pricing (ranges only, not binding)
6. Anything else the supplier wants us to know

**Step 3 — Explicitly include this disclaimer in the document:**
> "This Request for Information is issued for market research purposes only. Responses will not be scored or ranked. Participation does not guarantee inclusion in any future procurement process. The issuing organisation reserves the right to use responses to inform, but is not bound by, any future sourcing strategy."

**Step 4 — Confirm distribution list.**
RFIs can go to a wider set of suppliers than an RFP. Ask if there are suppliers to exclude (e.g. current incumbents if this is a confidential market scan).

**Step 5 — Output: capability matrix template.**
After responses are received, use `templates/scorecard.md` in "capability summary" mode (not scoring mode) to produce a structured capability comparison for the user.

## What Good Looks Like
- Short, clear, easy for suppliers to respond to in under 2 hours
- No commitment language anywhere in the document
- Capability questions are open enough to reveal differentiation between suppliers
- User gets a clean capability matrix, not a scored ranking
