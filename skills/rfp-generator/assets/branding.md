# Molecule One — Branding Reference for Document Generation

## Document Headers
- Organisation: use `org_name` from config.json
- All documents include: "Part of the Molecule One Claude Plugin Suite"
- Footer: "Confidential — [ORG_NAME] Procurement | moleculeone.ai"

## Tone of Voice
- Direct and professional — no filler phrases ("please note that...", "it should be mentioned...")
- Active voice — "The supplier will deliver" not "Delivery will be made by the supplier"
- Plain language — no jargon without explanation
- Precise — use specific dates, amounts, and requirements; avoid vague language

## Colour Palette (for Word document generation)
- Primary navy: #1B3A6B
- Accent blue: #2E75B6
- Light blue (table headers): #D5E8F0
- Body text: #1A1A1A

## Terminology Standards
- "Supplier" not "vendor" (unless the client uses vendor)
- "Proposal" not "bid" or "tender response"
- "Evaluation" not "assessment" or "scoring exercise"
- "Award" not "win" — procurement awards contracts, suppliers don't win them
- "Shortlist" not "short-list" or "final list"
- "Statement of Work" or "SOW" — not "spec" or "requirements doc"
- "Total Cost of Ownership" or "TCO" — always spell out on first use
- "Request for Proposal" not "Tender" (unless jurisdiction uses "tender" formally)

## Document Naming Convention
- RFP: [ORG_NAME]-RFP-[YYYY]-[NNN]-[CATEGORY].docx
- RFI: [ORG_NAME]-RFI-[YYYY]-[NNN]-[CATEGORY].docx
- RFQ: [ORG_NAME]-RFQ-[YYYY]-[NNN]-[CATEGORY].docx
- Scorecard: [RFP-REF]-Evaluation-Scorecard.docx
- Recommendation: [RFP-REF]-Award-Recommendation.docx
