# Molecule One — Brand & Terminology Standards
## Spend Intelligence Analyzer

### Tone
- Data-led. Numbers first, interpretation second.
- Confident but precise — use ranges, not false precision.
- Executive-grade: clear, brief, no jargon without explanation.
- Molecule One is an operator, not an advisor. We do the work, not just the recommendation.

### Key Terminology
- "Supplier" not "vendor"
- "Spend" not "expenditure" or "payments" (unless referring to a specific payment event)
- "Category" not "commodity" (commodity implies direct materials — most of our work is indirect)
- "Savings opportunity" not "saving" until it's delivered
- "Savings delivered" only after a contract is signed and the saving is locked in
- "Addressable spend" — the portion of spend that can realistically be affected by an action
- "Spend under management" — spend covered by a current contract or preferred supplier arrangement
- "Maverick spend" — spend outside contracted channels (not "off-contract spend" or "rogue spend")
- "Tail spend" — suppliers below a materiality threshold (define the threshold in every report)
- "PO compliance" not "purchase order compliance"

### Numbers & Figures
- Always use £/$/€ + comma separator for thousands: £1,250,000 not £1250000
- Round to nearest £1,000 for amounts over £100k; nearest £100 for amounts under £100k
- Percentages to 1 decimal place: 34.2%, not 34.2345% or "roughly 34%"
- Savings as ranges: £1.2M–£1.8M not £1,542,000
- Always state the basis: "(based on benchmark comparison)" or "(consolidation scenario)"
- Annualised figures always labelled: "annualised estimate (9 months × 12/9)"

### Document Naming
Spend Intelligence: `[ORG_NAME]-Spend-Intelligence-[PERIOD]-[YYYYMMDD].docx`
Opportunity Register: `[ORG_NAME]-Opportunity-Register-[YYYYMMDD].xlsx`
Category Heatmap: `[ORG_NAME]-Spend-Heatmap-[PERIOD].xlsx`
